<p>On 3 July, our Secondary 4 and 5 students came together for the Sports
Fiesta: Ultimate Frisbee Tournament, enjoying an afternoon of friendly
competition before beginning their final preparations for the national
examinations. The inter-class tournament provided the students with an
opportunity to strengthen their teamwork and camaraderie while putting
the Ultimate Frisbee skills they had learnt during Physical Education (PE)
lessons into practice. Working together in their class teams, students
demonstrated sharp passing, quick movement and effective communication
as they competed in a series of exciting matches. Throughout the tournament,
they displayed resilience, determination and good sportsmanship, creating
a positive and encouraging atmosphere on and off the field.</p>
<p>More than just a sporting event, the Sports Fiesta gave the students valuable
opportunity to relax, bond with their classmates and create lasting memories
before embarking on the next stage of their academic journey. The school
would also like to thank the PE Department and our Secondary 2 and 3 CCA
leaders for their dedication in organising a smooth and enjoyable event.
We wish our graduating cohort every success in their upcoming national
examinations!</p>
