<p>Our Secondary 1 students began Term 3 with an unforgettable Outdoor Adventure
Learning (OAL) Cohort Camp at the MOE Changi Coast Outdoor Adventure Learning
Centre (OALC). Over four days and three nights, they stepped beyond their
comfort zones, forged new friendships and discovered the value of resilience
and teamwork through a series of exciting outdoor experiences.</p>
<p>Preparation for the camp began in the middle of Term 2 during Physical
Education lessons, where students learnt essential outdoor skills such
as map reading and navigation, tent pitching and expedition-style cooking.
These skills came to life as students applied them in authentic outdoor
settings throughout the camp.</p>
<p>From kayaking across open waters to navigating the artificial caving course,
every activity challenged students to communicate effectively, trust one
another and persevere in the face of unfamiliar situations. Students also
took charge of preparing meals during an outdoor cooking session, working
together to plan, cook and share their food while developing cooperation,
creativity and problem-solving skills.</p>
<p>A highlight of the camp was the full-day land expedition through East
Coast Park and the Siglap area. Armed with maps and their newly acquired
navigation skills, students planned their routes, supported one another
and completed the journey together, demonstrating that success is built
on teamwork and mutual encouragement.</p>
<p>More than an outdoor adventure, the camp was a meaningful journey of growth.
Through every challenge and shared experience, students developed confidence,
resilience and responsibility while forging friendships that will continue
to shape their secondary school years. The memories created and lessons
learnt will undoubtedly remain with them as they embark on the next chapter
of their Regent journey.</p>
