<p>We are delighted to announce that Regent Secondary School has been accorded
the NorthWest Outstanding School Partner Award (Gold), in recognition of
our school's steadfast dedication to community involvement and meaningful
partnerships. The award was presented at the NorthWest Volunteers' Awards
ceremony held at the Singapore University of Social Sciences (SUSS) on
23 May 2026.</p>
<p>We are equally proud to celebrate with Muhammad Khairy Danish Bin Rozaini
from 4-Pride, who was honoured with the NorthWest Outstanding All-Rounder
Student Award for his outstanding contributions to the community. He received
the award accompanied by his proud parents – a moment that reflects the
values we strive to nurture in every Regenite.</p>
<p>We extend our heartfelt appreciation to the NorthWest Community Development
Council for their invaluable partnership and continued support. Through
this collaboration, our students have been empowered to take meaningful
steps towards becoming transformational leaders, enriching their learning
journeys both within and beyond the classroom.</p>
<p>This achievement belongs to the entire Regent community — our students,
staff, parents, and partners — and we celebrate it together with great
pride.</p>
