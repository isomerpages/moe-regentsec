<p>As part of this year’s Racial Harmony Day celebrations, the theme “Our
People, Our Tapestry” encouraged students to deepen their appreciation
of Singapore’s multicultural heritage and the values that unite our diverse
communities.</p>
<p>Through a range of interactive learning experiences, students explored
the stories, traditions, and cultures that have shaped Singapore’s unique
identity. During their Character and Citizenship Education (CCE) lessons,
students visited a Virtual Heritage Gallery curated on Padlet. Each level
explored a specially designed gallery featuring festive celebrations, traditional
attire, local cuisines, cultural practices, and interesting facts about
Singapore’s different communities. These activities helped students develop
a deeper understanding of the importance of respecting cultural diversity
and recognising the contributions of every community to Singapore’s shared
identity.</p>
<p>Students also reflected on how their perspectives had grown and considered
meaningful ways to build stronger friendships across different races and
cultures. Adding to the celebrations, the Parent Support Group (PSG) organised
interactive cultural booths where students experienced traditional games,
participated in hands-on handicraft activities, and sampled a variety of
local delicacies.</p>
<p>Through these engaging experiences, Racial Harmony Day aims to inspire
students to embrace diversity, strengthen mutual understanding and contribute
towards building a more caring, inclusive and united Singapore.</p>
